# Changelog

Notable changes to Freightline, newest first. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Accept offers from the job board: choose an offer, pick an idle van that can carry it, and see the job under In progress with its ETA.

- A job board beside the map, listing open offers with route, cargo, pay, distance, and deadline, sortable by pay, distance, or deadline.

- Pause and speed controls now appear in the top bar.

- Markers for your depot and nearby towns on the map, with town names on hover.

- A top bar now shows the name of the company, the date, and cash.

- A map of Plymouth and the surrounding coast.

### Fixed

- The land map is now filled in light green.
